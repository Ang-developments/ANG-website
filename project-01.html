Santa Maria House

Location
Santa Maria, Paros
Type
Private residence

Designed as a natural extension of the terrain, this residence in Santa Maria, Paros, follows the lines of the plot and blends seamlessly into the Cycladic landscape. Local stone recovered during the excavation process was reused throughout the exterior walls, giving the building a unique character while strengthening its relationship with the site. The result is a contemporary yet timeless composition that balances simplicity, material authenticity, and respect for its surroundings.

01 / 05

Reusing stone recovered on site ties the house to the ground it occupies. The masonry follows the terrain, while white plaster sets the texture of the stone in relief.

Recessed windows, garden paths and a shaded terrace connect the house to the landscape. From the courtyard to the roofline, the design keeps the focus on the materials and the changing Cycladic light.
