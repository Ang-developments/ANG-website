Casa Botanica

Location
Lagadia, Antiparos
Type
Three villas

Set in Lagadia on Antiparos, Casa Botanica looks across the Aegean toward Paros. Its three villas take their names from the island’s flora: Oregano, Thyme, and Heather & Sage. Rounded white walls catch the sun, while rough stone steps and low garden walls trace paths between olive trees and herbs.

01 / 15

Inside, pale timber, woven pendant lights, and built-in seating soften the white rooms. Wide doors open directly onto terraces and pools, so a morning coffee or a meal can move easily outdoors. Each villa has its own relationship with the landscape, from Oregano’s rooftop terrace to Thyme’s poolside studio and the upstairs balcony at Heather & Sage.
